About Mind Matters

How the workshop was built.

Mind Matters is an educational wellbeing initiative built around a single, practical workshop for Year 3–6 students.

It wasn't designed from assumptions. We reviewed Australian research on primary-aged students, then interviewed Year 3–6 teachers about what conflict actually looks like in their classrooms before designing anything. The workshop's four steps come directly from the skills those teachers named.

The finished prototype was then sent back to teachers and to a Year 3 student for honest review — and we're changing it based on what they said.

How it came together

  1. Reviewed Australian research on primary-aged students
  2. Interviewed Year 3–6 teachers about real classroom conflict
  3. Built a two-hour workshop with worksheets, games and scenarios
  4. Had teachers and a student review it honestly
  5. Committed to six changes based on that feedback

See the research, the feedback and what we're changing.

Questions

Common questions from schools

How long is the workshop?

Approximately two hours. We can discuss how that's scheduled around your timetable — including whether it runs as one block or is split.

What year levels is it suitable for?

Years 3 to 6. The content, language and activities are written specifically for this age group. Mixed groups across those year levels work well.

Can you deliver it online?

Yes. The core content is the same, with activities adapted so students are still participating rather than just watching. Online delivery runs through your school's preferred video platform.

How many students can take part?

Group sizes are flexible, from a single class up to a full year level. Pricing is structured in bands based on participant numbers — see the pricing page.

Is this a counselling or mental health service?

No. Mind Matters is an educational skill-building workshop. It doesn't provide counselling, assessment or clinical support, and it isn't a substitute for your school's wellbeing team or external services.

Do teachers need to prepare anything?

No preparation is required. We'll confirm the space, equipment and student numbers beforehand, and a staff member stays with the group in line with your school's policies.

What's the difference between in-person and online pricing?

In-person delivery has a higher minimum workshop cost because it includes on-site facilitation and printed materials. Online delivery removes those costs, so the minimum is lower. The content covered is the same.

When does the printing charge apply?

Printing applies at $5 per participant where printed worksheets and activity resources are required. For online sessions, digital resources can be used instead.

Is the booking fee separate from the workshop cost?

Yes. The $30 booking fee applies to every confirmed booking and sits on top of the workshop component.

Can pricing be adjusted for our school?

Pricing and workshop options may be customised depending on school requirements. If your situation doesn't fit neatly into the bands above, get in touch and we'll work something out.
